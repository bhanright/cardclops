Cardclops

A free program for browsing, searching and understanding your Magic: The Gathering
collection and decks. It runs entirely on your computer: nothing is uploaded, and
there is no account.

WHAT IT DOWNLOADS, AND ONLY WHEN YOU ASK
  - Card data and images from Scryfall (scryfall.com).
  - Optional price history from MTGJSON (mtgjson.com).
  - Decks from Archidekt, if you choose to import them from there.
The first-run setup asks before downloading anything. Automatic daily updates and
price-alert notifications are off until you turn them on.

WHERE YOUR DATA GOES
  - Your collection, decks, price history and settings: Documents\Cardclops
    (backed up there after each update). Uninstalling leaves this folder alone.
  - Card data and images (cache, safe to delete): %LOCALAPPDATA%\Cardclops
